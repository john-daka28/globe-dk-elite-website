# Premium Education Website Design References

## Scope
These references are used for information architecture and interaction inspiration only. No university copy, logos, or proprietary visual assets are being copied into GlobeDk.

## Harvard University
Official homepage: https://www.harvard.edu/

Observed principles:

- A concise welcome statement establishes the institution before deeper exploration.
- Large content areas are organized into clear editorial modules such as academics, campus, history, faculty, news, and student life.
- The homepage uses proof-oriented sections and measurable highlights to turn institutional breadth into scannable content.
- Global navigation groups content into a small number of meaningful categories instead of exposing every destination at once.

## Stanford University
Official homepage: https://www.stanford.edu/

Observed principles:

- The opening statement is short, confident, and values-led.
- The page uses strong section storytelling, alternating between mission, news, research, education, and community.
- Editorial cards and featured stories create a premium magazine-like rhythm.
- Large section headings and generous whitespace make long pages feel intentional rather than crowded.

## University of Oxford
Official homepage: https://www.ox.ac.uk/

Observed principles:

- The first interaction is task-oriented: the visitor can quickly begin a course search or an admissions journey.
- The homepage separates current news, study pathways, institutional stories, and discovery content into distinct modules.
- Personalised attention and academic support are elevated as a core value proposition.
- The design combines heritage, trust, and modern task completion instead of relying only on decorative effects.

## Direction for GlobeDk
The premium direction for GlobeDk is a modern academic editorial system: deep navy and warm orange retained as brand anchors, a compact task-focused hero, generous section spacing, high-contrast typography, proof-oriented cards, and clear learning pathways. The existing content remains intact; it is reorganized into a more deliberate sequence so the homepage feels distinctive without borrowing another institution's identity.

## GlobeDk implementation verification
The premium redesign was captured at 1440px desktop and 390px mobile widths. Desktop presents an editorial split hero, restrained academic typography, warm-paper sections, navy subject pathways, and a warm orange final CTA. Mobile stacks the hero image below the message, keeps the primary actions visible, and stacks the subject, learning-format, admissions, and footer modules without visible clipping.

The revised hierarchy retains the existing GlobeDk copy and content themes while presenting them through clearer modules. The visual direction is inspired by the referenced universities' use of institutional storytelling, section rhythm, task-oriented navigation, and proof-oriented content, but it does not reuse their identity or assets.
